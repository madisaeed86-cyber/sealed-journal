Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version

Your journal pages and checklists are encrypted and stay on your device. We never receive them. Free users see ads from Google, which uses cookies. If you buy Premium, Razorpay handles your payment.

Your journal

Your pages, checklists, moods, fonts and stickers are stored in your browser's storage on your own device. They are encrypted with a key made from your PIN before they are saved. Your PIN itself is never stored. We cannot read, recover or reset your journal, and we cannot recover a forgotten PIN.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdSense on the landing page and on the journal's home and checklist screens. Ads are never shown on the page you write in, and advertisers never receive your journal content. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, which may include personalised ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Premium users don't see ads.

Payments (Premium)

Premium payments are processed by Razorpay. Razorpay collects the details needed to take your payment, such as your phone number, email and payment method, under its own privacy policy. Our payment server only receives the order and payment IDs needed to confirm your purchase and create your Premium code. It doesn't store your card or UPI details, and it never sees your journal.

Things stored on your device

Besides your encrypted journal, the app stores your theme choice, your last-used font, the date of your last backup and, if you bought Premium, your Premium code. These stay on your device.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which lets Google see your IP address. The website host ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) and our payment server host (Railway) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and request times.

Backups

If you use "Download backup", an encrypted file is saved to your device. Where you keep that file is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use "Erase journal" on the lock screen, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. Both remove everything from your device.

Children

Sealed Journal is not directed at children under 13.
